Hi! I'm Mario, I'm a researcher in games, HCI, computational creativity, and digital arts. I'm also a game developer, with a focus on 3D art creation, including modeling, rigging, and animation. I'm very interested in music co-creation and the dynamic generation of assets for games and interactive experiences, and I'm also interested in the ongoing dialogue between people and interactive systems, and in how these systems can foster creativity, learning, collaboration, and collective reasoning.

About

About me

My work is at the intersection of game design, digital arts, and HCI. I did my PhD at Lancaster University and later joined UC Santa Cruz as a postdoctoral researcher, where I worked on NSF-funded research exploring serious games and community resilience. I’m part of the Game User Interaction and Intelligence Lab (GUII Lab), working with Professor Magy Seif El-Nasr. Alongside my research, I enjoy being involved in the broader games and HCI community: I publish and review regularly, and I serve as an Associate Editor for IEEE Transactions on Games. Across these different roles, I’m especially interested in how interactive systems can open up new possibilities for creativity, learning, collaboration, and decision-making.

Meu trabalho está na interseção entre game design, artes digitais e IHC. Fiz meu doutorado na Lancaster University e depois fui para a UC Santa Cruz como pesquisador de pós-doutorado, onde trabalhei em pesquisas financiadas pela NSF sobre serious games e resiliência comunitária. Continuo fazendo parte do Game User Interaction and Intelligence Lab (GUII Lab), trabalhando com a Professora Magy Seif El-Nasr. Além da pesquisa, gosto de participar ativamente da comunidade de games e IHC: publico e atuo regularmente como revisor, e também sou Associate Editor do IEEE Transactions on Games. Entre essas diferentes atividades, tenho especial interesse em como sistemas interativos podem abrir novas possibilidades para criatividade, aprendizagem, colaboração e tomada de decisão.

Research interests

A

Interactive media & computational creativity

Games, learning & societal challenges

How games and interactive experiences can support learning, reflection, preparedness, and engagement with complex societal challenges.
