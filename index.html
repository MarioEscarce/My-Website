About

About me

My work is at the intersection of game design, digital arts, and HCI. I did my PhD at Lancaster University and later joined UC Santa Cruz as a postdoctoral researcher, where I worked on NSF-funded research on serious games and community resilience. I’m part of the Game User Interaction and Intelligence Lab (GUII Lab), working with Professor Magy Seif El-Nasr. I also publish and review regularly in games and HCI, and serve as an Associate Editor for IEEE Transactions on Games.

Meu trabalho está na interseção entre game design, artes digitais e IHC. Fiz meu doutorado na Lancaster University e, posteriormente, fui pesquisador de pós-doutorado na UC Santa Cruz, onde trabalhei em pesquisas financiadas pela NSF sobre jogos sérios e resiliência comunitária. Faço parte do Game User Interaction and Intelligence Lab (GUII Lab), trabalhando com a Professora Magy Seif El-Nasr. Também publico e atuo regularmente como revisor nas áreas de jogos e IHC, e sou Editor Associado da IEEE Transactions on Games.

Research

Research interests

A

Games, game design & learning

Research on games and game design, including serious games, game-based learning, and the use of games to support learning, reflection, and engagement.

B

HCI, interactive media & computational creativity

Human-computer interaction and interactive systems, with interests in computational creativity, music co-creation, generative systems, and experiences that explore sound as a primary form of interaction and expression.

C

Collaborative interaction & decision-making

How the design of interactive systems shapes communication, collaboration, negotiation, and shared decision-making.

Selected projects

Research artifacts, games, and interactive experiments.

Art Game · Computational Creativity

Jikan to Kukan

An art game in which exploring and interacting with a virtual environment contributes to the emergence of music, allowing players to implicitly collaborate in shaping a shared musical experience.

Serious Games · HCI · Collaborative Learning

Discourse Analysis

Research on how player discourse, negotiation, and shared decision-making unfold in serious games for wildfire preparedness and community resilience.

VR · Interactive Musical System

Bubble Sounds

A virtual-reality musical instrument that uses color-to-tone translation and meta-interactivity, allowing users to generate music through visual elements and create relaxing harmonic progressions without requiring musical expertise.
