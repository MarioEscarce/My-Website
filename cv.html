Profile

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from Lancaster University and was recently a postdoctoral researcher at the University of California, Santa Cruz, where I contributed to NSF-funded research on serious games and community resilience. My work sits at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction, Procedural Content Generation, Game Design, computational creativity, and digital arts, with a particular interest in creativity, learning, collaboration, and collective decision-making.
